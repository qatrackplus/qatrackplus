# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

QATrack+ is a medical physics tool used at facilities around the world. The
people who build and use it come from many countries, backgrounds, and levels
of technical experience. Some contributors are here for their first open-source
experience. Some users are posting in their second or third language.

We pledge to make participation in this project and community a welcoming
experience for everyone, regardless of age, background, disability, ethnicity,
level of experience, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or
gender identity.

## Our Standards

**We ask everyone to:**

- Assume good faith. Treat every comment, issue, and pull request as coming
  from someone who genuinely wants to help or be helped.
- Be patient and kind, especially with newcomers or non-native English speakers.
- Give and receive feedback constructively. Critique the code or idea, not the
  person.
- Accept that maintainers have limited time and must prioritise. A slow or
  absent response is not a slight.
- Keep discussions on-topic and relevant to QATrack+.

**We will not tolerate:**

- Harassment, insults, or personal attacks in any form.
- Discriminatory jokes or language.
- Sustained disruption of discussions or reviews.
- Publishing someone's private information without permission.
- Any conduct that would reasonably be considered unprofessional in a healthcare
  or academic workplace.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies to all project spaces — GitHub Issues, Pull
Requests, Discussions, the
[Google Group](https://groups.google.com/g/qatrack), and any other forum
where community members represent QATrack+.

## Enforcement

If you witness or experience unacceptable behaviour, please report it by
emailing the project maintainer:

**[medphys@crcrewso.ca](mailto:medphys@crcrewso.ca)**

Reports are handled confidentially. The maintainer will review the situation
and respond as promptly as possible. Consequences may range from a private
reminder to a temporary or permanent ban, depending on the severity.

If your report concerns the maintainer directly, please reach out to another
long-standing contributor listed in
[CONTRIBUTORS.txt](CONTRIBUTORS.txt).
